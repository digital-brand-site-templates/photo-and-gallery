About Por-trayal

Por-trayal is an independent gallery for original art. We represent painters, illustrators and photographers whose work deserves a wall rather than a warehouse, and we sell it directly to the people who will live with it.

Every piece in the collection is chosen by hand. We meet the artists, we see the work in person, and we only list a piece when we would happily hang it ourselves. That is why the catalogue stays small and changes often.

Prints are produced in limited runs on archival paper, signed and numbered by the artist. Originals are sold once and never reproduced. Whichever you choose, it arrives framed, insured and ready to hang.

What we stand for

Artists first

Artists set their own prices and keep the larger share of every sale. No exclusivity clauses, no long lock-ins.

Documented provenance

Each work ships with a signed certificate recording the artist, the year, the materials and the edition number.

Delivered properly

Crated by hand, fully insured in transit, and free on orders over $199. If it arrives damaged we replace it.
